## Overview

Kumo is an independent desktop software project built around a simple idea: your desktop experience can feel more alive without becoming distracting.

Inspired by the concept of contextual desktop interactions, Kumo introduces a small, persistent interface that lives at the top of your screen and expands when you need it.

Rather than being another application that demands your attention, Kumo aims to become a subtle companion that naturally fits into your everyday workflow.

From system awareness to productivity and media interactions, the goal is to bring useful experiences into one small, cohesive space.

## The Idea Behind Kumo

Modern desktops are filled with applications, notifications, and separate utilities.

Kumo explores a different approach.

What if some of those everyday interactions could happen in one small place, without constantly switching between windows?

That's where Kumo begins.

**Small in appearance. Meaningful in interaction.**

## What We're Building

Kumo is being developed around several core experiences:

| Experience | Description |
|:--|:--|
| **Desktop Presence** | A compact, persistent interface designed to feel like a natural part of your desktop. |
| **System Awareness** | Contextual feedback for everyday system activities and device states. |
| **Music Experience** | A more expressive way to interact with music, playback, and lyrics. |
| **Temporary Shelf** | A convenient space for temporarily keeping files and frequently used items. |
| **Focus & Productivity** | Lightweight productivity experiences designed to support focused work. |

These areas represent the direction of development and may evolve as Kumo progresses.

## Design Philosophy

Kumo follows a few principles that guide its development.

- **Minimal by Nature** — Useful interactions should not require unnecessary visual complexity.
- **Contextual by Design** — Information should appear when it matters, not constantly compete for attention.
- **Motion with Purpose** — Animations should communicate state, interaction, and personality.
- **One Cohesive Experience** — Different capabilities should feel like parts of the same product.
- **A Little Personality** — Software can be functional while still feeling warm and approachable.
